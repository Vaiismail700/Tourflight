Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Tourflight flight search website. Contact: ismailtasheel4@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Search information: the airports, dates, currency and filters you choose, so we can show results.
  • Technical information: such as IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and clicks on partner links, collected through our hosting and server logs.
  • Account information: if you register in future, your name, email and saved flights.

We do not take payments and we do not ask for or store card details or passport details.

3. How we use it

To provide and improve the Service, show relevant prices and currency, keep the Service secure, count clicks on partner links, and meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service (for example hosting and the flight price data provider), and as required by law. When you click a partner link, the Partner Site may receive information such as a referral identifier and your browser details, and its own privacy policy then applies. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies and local storage

Tourflight uses one small first-party cookie, called tf_consent, to remember the cookie choices you make in our cookie banner. It lasts about six months and does not identify you. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

Tourflight loads an optional third-party affiliate script when you allow advertising in Cookie settings. Changing your choice stops the script from loading on your next page visit.

Our hosting provider records technical logs such as IP address and pages requested. Some content on our pages is loaded from other services (for example airline logos), and when you click a partner link, the partner site may set its own cookies under its own policy.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable technical measures to protect it, but no online service is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object or withdraw consent. Contact ismailtasheel4@gmail.com to make a request.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries. We take reasonable steps to protect it when it is transferred.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
