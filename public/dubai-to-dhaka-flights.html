Dubai to Dhaka Flights

Compare one-way and round-trip flight options from Dubai (DXB) to Dhaka (DAC) for your travel dates.

Open flight search

On the search page, enter DXB for departure and DAC for arrival. Choose your dates and compare the available offers.

Airports and onward travel

Dubai International → Hazrat Shahjalal International (DXB → DAC)

Compare non-stop and connecting options by arrival time as well as fare. If relatives are collecting you in Dhaka, share the flight number and allow extra time for baggage collection.

What to check before booking

If your final stop is outside Dhaka, price the onward trip separately. For a separately booked domestic flight, allow time to clear immigration and recheck baggage.

Planning your trip to Dhaka

If you plan to continue from Dhaka to another city, compare an onward flight with a separate ground transfer. Leave time for immigration and baggage collection before a separate booking.

Before you book, check the fare with your baggage included, the total travel time and the provider's change rules. Booking and payment take place on the provider's website.
