Abu Dhabi to Dhaka Flights

Compare one-way and round-trip flight options from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Dhaka (DAC) for your travel dates.

Open flight search

On the search page, enter AUH for departure and DAC for arrival. Choose your dates and compare the available offers.

Airports and onward travel

Zayed International → Hazrat Shahjalal International (AUH → DAC)

If you can depart from either Abu Dhabi or Dubai, check both airport codes on the same dates. Include the cost of getting to the departure airport and getting home again on a return trip.

What to check before booking

For a family trip, compare the final fare for the full group after adding checked bags. Baggage rules can differ between two offers for the same flight.

Planning your trip to Dhaka

If you live between Abu Dhabi and Dubai, compare the total trip cost from both airports, including the ride to the airport and any checked bags.

Before you book, check the fare with your baggage included, the total travel time and the provider's change rules. Booking and payment take place on the provider's website.
