About Us – Tourflight

Explore • Compare • Fly

Welcome to Tourflight, your trusted platform for discovering, comparing, and exploring flight options around the world.

At Tourflight, our mission is to make flight discovery easier, smarter, and more transparent. We help travellers compare available flight options from airlines and online travel agencies, allowing them to explore different routes, prices, schedules, and travel opportunities in one convenient place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify the way people search for flights by bringing travel information together through an easy-to-use digital platform.

We aim to help travellers make informed decisions by providing convenient access to flight comparisons and travel options.

What We Do

Tourflight provides:

  • International flight search and comparison.
  • Flight fare and schedule comparisons.
  • Access to airline and travel agency offers.
  • Links to third-party booking providers.
  • Travel information to help customers make informed choices.

How Tourflight Works

Tourflight operates as a flight metasearch and comparison platform.

When you search for a flight, we may display information and offers obtained from airlines, online travel agencies, and other travel service providers.

When you select an offer, you may be redirected to the relevant third-party provider's website to complete your booking and payment.

Tourflight does not directly issue flight tickets, process flight booking payments, or operate flights under its initial business model.

Our Business Model

Tourflight may generate revenue through affiliate partnerships, referral programmes, advertising, and other commercial arrangements with travel providers.

Some links on our platform may be affiliate links. This means we may receive a commission if you complete a qualifying booking through a partner link, without necessarily increasing the price you pay.

Our Commitment

We are committed to:

  • Transparency in flight comparisons.
  • Providing a convenient search experience.
  • Respecting user privacy.
  • Protecting personal information.
  • Improving our technology and services.
  • Helping travellers discover more opportunities around the world.

Important Information

Tourflight is an independent flight comparison platform and is not an airline, ticket issuer, or travel operator.

Flight availability, fares, booking conditions, baggage allowances, refunds, and cancellations are determined by the relevant airline or booking provider.
