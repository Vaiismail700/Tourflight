Dubai to London Flights

Compare one-way and round-trip flight options from Dubai (DXB) to London (LHR) for your travel dates.

Open flight search

On the search page, enter DXB for departure and LHR for arrival. Choose your dates and compare the available offers.

Airports and onward travel

Dubai International → London Heathrow (DXB → LHR)

This guide uses Heathrow. If you also compare Gatwick or other London airports, check the onward fare and travel time to your actual destination before picking the cheapest flight.

What to check before booking

For a separate onward flight, check the airport code and terminal on both bookings. Allow time for a terminal or airport change and for baggage recheck.

Planning your trip to London

This page focuses on Heathrow. If you compare other London airports, consider the cost and time of getting from each airport to your final address.

Before you book, check the fare with your baggage included, the total travel time and the provider's change rules. Booking and payment take place on the provider's website.
